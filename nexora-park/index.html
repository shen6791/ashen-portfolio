Scan the plate, tap once

Agents photograph the number plate. The app finds the plate in the photo, reads it on the phone and checks it against Sri Lankan plate formats.

  • Finds small, distant plates by locating the bright plate shape first, then enlarging it
  • Fixes common misreads such as O/0, I/1, S/5 and B/8 by position
  • Knows the car from past visits: pre-selects its type and flags unpaid fines
  • Free and private: no API costs, and the photo never leaves the phone

The right fee, and no more drive-offs

At check-out the fee is worked out from the price chart, and any fine from an earlier drive-off is added with one tick.

  • Every started hour counts, with free grace minutes
  • "Left without paying" adds a fine to that plate
  • End-of-day close turns forgotten open tickets into fines
  • The receipt shows parking and past fines separately

Every ticket, every filter

Owners filter by date, plate, ticket, status, park, section, vehicle type and agent, then export to Excel.

  • Revenue, still parked, drive-offs and fines at a glance
  • Times shown in the business's own timezone
  • Close a forgotten ticket straight from the report

Know every vehicle

Type any plate to see how often it parks, how long it stays, what it has paid and whether it owes a fine.

  • Fuzzy matching: "cab1234" finds "WP CAB-1234"
  • Record unpaid fines as paid from the owner's side

A platform you can run as a business

New operators sign up for a free trial. The super-admin panel records payments, changes plans, extends trials, suspends accounts and keeps an audit log.

  • Plan limits on car parks and agents, enforced when adding them
  • Warning before expiry, then a grace period, then a paused gate
  • Data is never deleted; recording a payment restores access instantly

How it is built

One database, two products. Nexora Park shares a Supabase Postgres database with my POS product but lives in its own schema, with its own logins (shop code + username, since gate agents rarely have email). The schema is not exposed to the public API and has row-level security switched on with no policies, so only the server can reach it. Every query is scoped to the signed-in business, and the "one open ticket per plate" rule is enforced by a unique index as well as in code.
