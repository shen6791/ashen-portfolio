About

01 / PROFILE

I'm Ashen — an IT Assistant at Celogen Lanka, a pharmaceutical company in Sri Lanka, where I sit between the people running a process and the system that should be running it for them. Most of my work starts with a spreadsheet someone is tired of maintaining by hand: a field-force mileage log, a sample inventory count, a report that takes half a day to assemble. I turn it into a web app or an Apps Script automation, then stay on to support it.

I hold a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science, Software Engineering from the University of Wolverhampton and an HND in Computing from Pearson, both completed through CINEC Campus. Before Celogen, I worked IT support at SL Tech and Advertising and taught English at British Way English Academy — a mix that's made me equally comfortable debugging a script and explaining it to someone who's never seen one.
