A POS screen built for speed

Tap products or scan barcodes. Each line shows its working, and stock levels update after every sale.

  • Cash, card, multiple payments and credit sales
  • Drafts, quotations and suspended sales
  • Print an 80mm receipt or a full A4 invoice

Every shop, its own look

Shop owners upload their logo, pick a theme or their own colours, and choose an invoice design — with a live preview of the real invoice.

  • 8 themes plus custom primary, sidebar and accent colours
  • Classic, Modern, Minimal and Bold invoice designs
  • Logo size and position, bank details, terms, signature lines
  • Nothing changes for staff until the owner presses Publish

Invoices and receipts that look professional

The same branding flows into every A4 invoice and 80mm receipt.

Stock you can trust

FIFO costing: each sale takes stock from the oldest batch first and uses that batch's cost, so your profit figures are real.

  • See open batches, what's left and which is sold next
  • Full stock history for every product
  • Transfers, adjustments, stock counts and low-stock alerts

A platform you can run as a business

The super-admin panel manages every shop: plans, feature access, payments, support tickets, announcements and an audit log.

  • Reminder the day before expiry, then a grace period
  • Unpaid shops are restricted automatically — data is never deleted
  • Recording a payment restores access instantly
